## 01  USA Workforce Snapshot

Subtitle: Interactive labor-market atlas for every U.S. state and major metro
Date: September 2026

## Description

The Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes official jobs, unemployment, earnings and price data for every state and metropolitan area, every month, for free. In its published form it is spread across four programs and thousands of coded series, and it takes a statistician to turn it into an answer. USA Workforce Snapshot turns the latest release into one map. Click any state or metropolitan area and you get who is working, what they do, and whether their pay is keeping up with prices, each figure set against the nation, the state and the same month a year earlier. It refreshes itself the day after every BLS release. It is free, open source, and built for workforce boards, economic development offices and nonprofits who need the numbers without the pipeline.

Open the dashboard: workforce.usllab.org
Source code: github.com/cyber-hbliu/workforce_demographic_dashboard

## Screenshot notes

Take screenshots from workforce.usllab.org in a desktop browser at 1440 px wide or wider, with the page zoomed to 100%, so the Source Code Pro typeface and the real July 2026 figures appear. For the square and tall slots, crop rather than resize. Export as PNG at 2x if the browser allows it.
